Privacy Policy for Hathia

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data collection (none)

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, health data, or device information. This matters especially here: cycle and symptom data is sensitive, and it never leaves your device.

2. Local storage only

Everything you enter — period days, symptoms, health entries (temperature, weight, sleep, water, cervical fluid, tests and similar), cycle settings, and a hashed copy of your app-lock PIN if you set one — is stored exclusively on your own device, in a local SQLite database (via the Drift database library), inside the app's private, sandboxed storage. We have no servers, no databases, and no access to your information.

3. No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, web beacons, fingerprinting, or any tracking technologies. The app makes no network requests except the one described below. The only persistent data on your device is your logs and settings.

4. Third-party services

We do not integrate with any analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. The only external service is Google Play, which processes your Hathia Pro purchase through the Google Play Billing Library. Buying Pro and restoring it go through it. We do not receive or store your payment details: those are handled entirely by Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that part. The app has no internet permission of its own.

5. Things you start that leave the app

The app never sends anything by itself. These hand over to another app only when you choose: exporting a CSV or a doctor summary (to the sharing app you pick), rating the app (four or five stars open the Google Play page so you can leave a public review; a lower rating with a comment opens an email draft to the developer, and the comment is only sent if you send that email, so please do not put health details in it).

6. Home screen widget, notifications and recent apps

The optional widget counts down the days to your next period. It is worked out on your phone from dates the app saves in its private storage, and nothing is sent anywhere. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it, so with Discreet notifications on the widget shows only a number, and reminders say nothing about your cycle. "Hide Hathia in recent apps" keeps the app out of the recent-apps list and screenshots. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so your data is not copied to the cloud or to a new phone.

7. Children's privacy

Hathia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age — consistent with Section 1, we collect no data at all.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / POPIA)

Because we hold zero data about you, there is nothing to access, rectify, or delete. All your data is under your physical control on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" inside Settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy — for example, adding an optional cloud feature — we will flag it clearly in the app. This version remains valid for the fully offline version of the app.
